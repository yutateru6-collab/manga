# Fictional Example: PocketRecall

This example uses a fictional product so no real-world claims are implied.

## Grounded fictional facts for the example

- PocketRecall is a fictional study app.
- A user can save a word and create a review card.
- The app can schedule a later review.
- No performance statistics or learning guarantees are assumed.

## Three distinct angles

### 1. Screenshot graveyard
- Segment: learners who save vocabulary screenshots but never revisit them
- Motivation: frustration with “collecting” instead of reviewing
- Hook visual: phone gallery filled with vocabulary screenshots
- Hook copy: 「保存した瞬間だけ、勉強した気になる。」
- Mechanism: turn one saved word into a review card
- Proof: direct UI demonstration only

### 2. Tomorrow test
- Segment: learners who recognize a word today but cannot recall it tomorrow
- Motivation: fear of false familiarity
- Hook visual: same word shown today vs blank recall tomorrow
- Mechanism: schedule a later recall prompt
- Proof: show the workflow, not a guaranteed score improvement

### 3. Five-minute identity
- Segment: busy learners who reject long study sessions
- Motivation: consistency/identity
- Hook visual: learner opens the app during a short train wait
- Mechanism: review one small set
- Proof: product interaction only

## Recommended 4-panel concept: Screenshot graveyard

### Panel 1 — Hook
Visual: phone gallery overflowing with saved vocabulary screenshots.
Copy: 「保存した瞬間だけ、勉強した気になる。」

### Panel 2 — Friction
Visual: several days later, the gallery is larger; the learner cannot remember which screenshots matter.
Copy: 「で、見返さない。」

### Panel 3 — Mechanism
Visual: one word being converted into a clean review card inside the fictional app.
Copy: 「“保存”を、“復習する形”に。」

### Panel 4 — Payoff + CTA
Visual: learner reviews a short card set while waiting for a train.
Copy: 「集めるだけで終わらせない。」
CTA: 「使い方を見る」

## Production note

Generate the four-panel art with clean reserved balloon/caption areas. Overlay the exact Japanese copy afterward. Do not generate fake ratings, download counts, score gains, or testimonials.
